I like a very broad range of music, some comes from my youth, some I have picked up over the years and some are very current but all are great tunes. I will update this periodically.

Here's my current favourite songs to listen to (no particular order):

{% include spotifyplaylist.html id=page.spotifyplaylist %}
<br>
<h4>National Health - The Bryden 2-Step (For Amphibians)</h4>
This song has been a favourite for some time, I'm always just fascinated by how good the band sounds, and the track sounds like one live take.
<h4>Rainbow - All Night Long</h4>
The inner crooner in me loves to sing this, I belt this one out whenever I see a tribute band play it.
<h4>Zopp - You</h4>
This is a new one but has the timeless sound of 1970s Canterbury prog
<h4>ELP - Tarkus</h4>
What an immense track, and I've already told Carl Palmer that I want this playing at my funeral! Tricky prog at its best, what a great keyboard player Keith Emerson was.
<h4>ELO - Livin' Thing</h4>
I started to get into ELO in the 1970's and this track is a classic, but I could have picked any from most of their albums
<h4>Status Quo - Living On An Island</h4>
Quo weren't a one trick pony and this track showcases a different style (and vocal) to what is usually played
<h4>Deep Purple - Sometimes I Feel Like Screaming </h4>
I like the way this builds from a quiet song and really showcases what a great vocalist Ian Gillan is.
<h4>Haze - Dig Them Mushrooms</h4>
Haze are a local prog band that have been going for over 40 years. This is an early classic and is one of my favourite keyboard tracks ever
<h4>Stackridge - Rufus T Firefly</h4>
I came across this as Frank Zappa was named as an influence. I like Stackridge in their quirky English usual form but this is something else, definitely Zappa-esque.
<h4>Queensryche - Revolution Calling</h4>
This is a song where you can really get on board with the lyrical message delivered in a great song. Who do you trust when everyone's a crook?
<h4>William Walton - Crown Imperial</h4>
I used to fall asleep to Classic FM which got me into this modern classic
<h4>Gillan - Men of War</h4>
Here's Gillan again but with his own band. He's at the top of his vocal game with this belter.
<h4>Lindisfarne - Run For Home</h4>
This is a great feelgood song from my youth, one of many 'pop' songs as we used to call them.
<h4>Black Sabbath - Die Young</h4>
Another from my younger days listening to Hallam Rock, Ronnie James Dio in great form with Black Sabbath. I also love Neon Knights!
<h4>PFM - Appena Un Po</h4>
This is my go to flight take off music with active noise cancellation. PFM are an Italian prog band who released some great albums, notably in the early 1970's, super-talented composers.
<h4>Frank Zappa - Son Of Mr Green Genes</h4>
I could have picked from many Zappa songs, he is often dismissed as some sort of zany character but Zappa was a very talented composer. On this track I love how it builds and the brass turns to guitar.
<h4>XTC - Wrapped in Grey</h4>
A vastly underrated band, this was going to be a serious single but was pulled unfortunately. A lovely melody and lyrical excellence, commonplace in any XTC song.
<h4>The Drifters - There Goes My First Love</h4>
I love the deep vocal on this one, the Drifters delivered many great tunes over the years and this reminds me of growing up
<h4>The Beach Boys - Lady Lynda</h4>
I'm a sucker for a singalong tune and the beach boys had many but for some reason this one has always been a favourite
